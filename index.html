What is Life Certificate?

A Life Certificate is an official document that confirms a pensioner is alive and eligible to continue receiving pension payments.

Earlier, pensioners had to visit banks or government offices every year. Today, eligible pensioners can generate a Digital Life Certificate through the Government of India's Jeevan Pramaan service.

Important: Always use the official Jeevan Pramaan portal and never share OTPs with unknown callers.

What is Jeevan Pramaan Life Certificate?

Jeevan Pramaan is an Aadhaar-based Digital Life Certificate service that allows eligible pensioners to generate their certificate electronically.

  • Uses Aadhaar authentication.
  • Provides a unique Pramaan ID.
  • Certificate is available digitally.
  • Accessible by participating pension authorities.

Jeevan Pramaan Life Certificate Download PDF

After generating your Digital Life Certificate, you can download the PDF using your Pramaan ID.

  1. Open the official Pensioner Login page.
  2. Enter your Pramaan ID.
  3. Verify OTP.
  4. Download the Life Certificate PDF.

How to Check Life Certificate Status

To check your Life Certificate status, use your Pramaan ID on the official Jeevan Pramaan portal.

  • Enter Pramaan ID.
  • Verify OTP.
  • View certificate.
  • Download PDF if available.
  • Contact your pension authority if rejected.

Offline Life Certificate Submission

Pensioners who cannot use online services can still submit their Life Certificate through banks, pension offices, or authorized service centers.
